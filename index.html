Dear Sweety,

We don’t really sit down and have big emotional conversations, mostly because five minutes into any serious talk one of us starts laughing or teasing the other. So I wanted to write this down where you can keep it and read it whenever you need a reminder.

Having you as my sister is genuinely one of the best parts of my life. When I look back at all our photos — from when you were tiny with a pink ribbon in your hair to watching you grow into the strong, warm, radiant person you are today — I feel so proud of you.

Thank you for being there in the small, ordinary moments: the random late-evening talks, the shared laughter over things nobody else finds funny, and the quiet comfort of just knowing you’re in my corner.

Life is going to take us through busy schedules, new cities, and big chapters, but I want you to be completely certain of one thing: no matter where you go or what you face, you always have a brother standing right behind you. You never have to carry heavy days alone.

You deserve a life filled with genuine peace, loud laughter, people who value your kindness, and every dream you’re brave enough to chase.

Always cheering for you,
